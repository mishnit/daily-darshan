Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 1/1/2026

At VIP Seva ("we," "us," or "our"), accessible from https://vipseva.com, protecting the privacy of our users is our top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we handle user privacy, data protection, and Meta business integration requirements.

1. Information Handling

We do not store any personal information on our servers or databases. Any operational data required during session interactions is processed purely for service execution and is not permanently retained.

2. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We value your privacy and maintain a strict non-tracking policy. We do not use any pixel tags (including Meta Pixel), web beacons, cookies, or any other tracking technologies to track or collect information about your browsing activity.

3. Meta Platform Notice

Meta Business & Developer Compliance: If you interact with VIP Seva through Meta (Facebook) integrations, your interaction complies with Meta’s Platform Terms and Developer Policies. We do not sell, share, or trade user data with any third parties except Meta.

4. Data Deletion and Protection Rights

We adhere to strict data protection practices regarding user privacy and data lifecycle:

  • No Personal Information Stored: We do not store any personal information.
  • Automatic Deletion After 3 Months of Inactivity: We automatically remove users' data after 3 months of inactivity.

Manual Data Deletion Request:

In addition to our automated data removal after 3 months of inactivity, users have the right to request immediate deletion of any temporary data at any time:

  • Email Request: Contact us directly at support@vipseva.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  • Facebook App Settings: If you linked through Facebook, you can revoke access and trigger data removal by navigating to Facebook Settings & Privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites, locating VIP Seva, and selecting Remove.

5. Security

We employ standard technical measures to safeguard our platform and ensure no unauthorized retention or handling of user information occurs.
