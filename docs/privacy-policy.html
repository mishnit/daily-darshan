Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

VIP Seva provides Daily Darshan subscriptions, WhatsApp updates, personalized pages, payment review, and optional sharing rewards. This policy explains what information these features use and what happens when you interact with them.

Information we handle

  • Subscription information: WhatsApp phone number, display name, chosen plan, subscription dates and status, consent choice, and subscription ID.
  • Messages and delivery: incoming WhatsApp message identifiers and content needed to respond, outgoing message identifiers, delivery status, and operational error or audit records.
  • Payments: payment reference, plan and amount, submitted UTR, verification decision and timestamps. If payment-gateway mode is enabled, we also handle gateway checkout and payment identifiers. The gateway handles card or bank payment details on its own service; VIP Seva does not ask you to send card details through WhatsApp.
  • Sharing and referrals: the referrer's phone number embedded in a referral link, a visitor's phone number when they contact us through that link, and daily Karma point records when a page's share action reports completion.
  • Website use: our hosting providers may process ordinary request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the site. We do not currently add our own advertising or analytics cookies to these pages.

How we use this information

We use it to create and manage subscriptions, respond to WhatsApp requests, send the Daily Darshan and subscription updates a person agreed to receive, review or confirm payments, show personalized pages, prevent duplicate messages or payment credit, calculate sharing rewards, and investigate delivery or payment problems.

WhatsApp choices

We request consent before enabling Daily Darshan messages. Reply STOP in WhatsApp to stop future subscription messages; you can choose to resume later. Stopping messages does not automatically cancel a paid plan or erase payment records. You can ask about your subscription by messaging the VIP Seva WhatsApp number.

Sharing and personalized links

A personalized page link should be treated as private even though the page is publicly accessible to someone who knows the link. Sharing the page may reveal the name, expiry, and Karma points shown on it. The Darshan share text includes a referral URL containing the subscriber's phone number; anyone receiving or forwarding that text can see it. Only use the share feature if you are comfortable disclosing that number.

Where information goes

We use Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform to receive and send messages, GitHub to store repository data and serve public pages and images, and Render to run the webhook service. If gateway payments are enabled, Razorpay processes the hosted checkout and sends payment status to our service. These providers process information under their own terms and policies.

Some operational records are currently held in a public GitHub repository. These may contain subscriber and referral phone numbers, names, subscription details, payment references and UTRs, and delivery identifiers. Public copies or repository history may remain accessible after the current records change. Personalized pages can be opened by anyone with their link and may show a name, subscription expiry, and Karma points.

Retention

Operational logs and the daily delivery ledger are configured for a 30-day cleanup window; images are configured to retain the newest seven days; subscriber pages remain while active and for a seven-day grace period after expiry. Subscription, consent, payment, referral, and Karma records are not currently covered by an automatic fixed deletion period. Records may also remain in Git history, backups, forks, or provider systems after they disappear from the current repository files.

Questions and requests

You may ask us to explain, correct, or delete information we control, or raise a privacy concern, subject to information we must retain for payment or legal reasons. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service or its data storage changes. The date at the top shows when this page was last revised.
